2. Hosting and server log data

This website is hosted by klxm.de, with servers located in Germany. Like virtually every web server, each request to this site is technically logged, typically including: the requesting IP address, date and time, the page requested, the referring page, browser and operating system information (user agent), and the amount of data transferred. This is processed to deliver the page, keep the service secure and stable, and diagnose faults, on the basis of a legitimate interest in operating the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR where applicable; Art. 31 DSG). These logs are kept for 30 days and are not merged with other data sources.

3. Cookies and local storage

This website does not set cookies and does not use browser local storage or similar persistent identifiers. The only client-side script on this page (js/lightbox.js) enlarges screenshots on click and stores nothing on your device. No consent banner is shown because no non-essential cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies are used. If that changes in the future, this policy and the site will be updated accordingly, with a consent mechanism added where required.

4. No analytics or tracking

This site does not use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel, embedded YouTube videos, marketing pixels, or any other third-party analytics or advertising service. Web fonts are self-hosted with this site's own files rather than loaded from Google Fonts, so no visitor data is sent to Google merely by viewing a page.

5. External links and downloads

This site links to third-party destinations, in particular GitHub (github.com/CarstenMaul/vividcreatorapp) and platform installer downloads. Once you follow such a link, the respective third party's own privacy policy applies to any data processed on their site or by their download infrastructure — this policy does not cover them.

6. International transfers

The controller is based in Switzerland; the website is hosted by klxm.de with servers located in Germany, within the EU/EEA. For visitors in the EU/EEA, server log data is processed inside the EEA and involves no cross-border transfer. For the transfer of data between Switzerland and Germany, no additional safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are required: the European Commission recognizes Switzerland as providing an adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR), and the Swiss Federal Council likewise recognizes the EU/EEA member states as adequate under the DSG.
